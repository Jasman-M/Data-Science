## Projects

### [Statistical Fairness Audit for Credit Models](./fairness-audit/)
`Python` `XGBoost` `scikit-learn` `Bootstrap CI`

Audits an ML credit scoring model for demographic bias across gender, education, and age. Computes Disparate Impact Ratio (EEOC 4/5 rule), Equal Opportunity Difference, Predictive Equality Difference, calibration curves, and bootstrap AUC confidence intervals — the multi-metric framework regulators increasingly expect under ECOA and fair lending law.

---

### [Loan Default Survival Analysis](./survival-analysis/)
`R` `Kaplan-Meier` `Cox Proportional Hazards` `Log-Rank Test`

Models *when* borrowers default — not just whether — on a synthetic cohort of 5,000 borrowers tracked over 24 months. Fits stratified KM curves by risk tier and education, runs log-rank tests, estimates a Cox PH model with hazard ratios, and validates the proportional hazards assumption via Schoenfeld residuals.
