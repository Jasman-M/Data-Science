How ARIA works
 

The assessment flow

ARIA follows a fixed eight-step underwriting conversation rather than free-form chat.

  • Identify the line of business, then intake the applicant profile
  • Probe vague or contradictory fields before scoring anything
  • Sort risk factors into elevated, mitigating, and unverified
  • Surface illustrative industry comparables and their usual outcomes
  • Recommend a tier, explain the two or three factors that drove it, and invite a what-if

Guardrails in the prompt

  • Pasted profiles are treated as data, so instructions hidden inside a submission are ignored and flagged
  • Fraud detection criteria are never enumerated, only applied
  • Repeated what-ifs that walk one variable toward a tier boundary are declined
  • Applicant identifiers are not echoed back, and no tier is ever a binding decision

Guardrails in this app

  • Messages are scanned for identifier patterns (SIN, SSN, health card, card numbers) before they leave the browser
  • The disclaimer and demo notice are shown on every new session
  • The worksheet is built from a structured block ARIA emits, so the panel can never claim a tier the prose did not
  • Nothing is stored on a server by this page; the session lives in this browser only
